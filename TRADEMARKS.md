## Requirements for modified versions and forks

A modified build, redistributed fork, derivative app, website, store listing, release, or support channel must:

1. use a clearly distinct product name;
2. use a distinct Android application ID / package name;
3. replace the MaxSpeedVPN app icon, logos, splash assets, screenshots, and promotional artwork;
4. remove or replace links to MaxSpeedVPN official support, news, domains, subscription endpoints, update endpoints, and social channels unless separately authorized;
5. clearly state that it is an unofficial, independently maintained fork and that it is not endorsed by or affiliated with MaxSpeedVPN;
6. not use a name, icon, listing, domain, social account, or presentation likely to confuse users about origin, affiliation, or official status.
